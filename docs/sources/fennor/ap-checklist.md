# Payables intake checklist

Fennor Community Services.
Issued February 12, 2025.
Owner: Orla Fen, Accounts assistant.

## 1. Inbox

- Keep the supplier's email and invoice together.
- Record the day the office received the bill.
- Capture supplier, property, invoice reference, invoice date, amount, and work.
- Preserve leading zeroes and prefixes. Trim spaces around the reference only.
- A reminder keeps the original invoice date and terms.

## 2. Existing entries

- Open **Invoices > Search**.
- Search the supplier number and invoice reference across all listed entries.
- Treat a matching entry as an already received bill.
- Attach the new email to that case and stop.

## 3. Validation

- Use **Vendors** to match the supplier and property.
- Check the email against its attachment.
- Ask the payables desk about missing records, ambiguous matches, or conflicting amounts.
- Resolve or assign an intake question within two business days.
- Leave the case open until it is resolved.

## 4. Entry and approval

- Check the insurance record and retain any payment hold.
- Enter the invoice in **New invoice** and retain its number.
- In **Approvals**, send the amount, work, due date, and holds to the property lead or board.
- Read their decisions in **Approvals > Decisions**.
- Keep a rejection or unanswered request out of the payment queue.
