# Finance desk notes

Fennor Community Services.
Updated March 3, 2025.

## 1. Supplier records

Supplier numbers are unique across our company.
An A entry is posted.
An X entry is void and does not block resubmission.
Retain the entry used for a duplicate check.
Use the entries available at receipt, not a later posting or cancellation.

## 2. Decisions and holds

The manager signs bills up to $5,000.
The board signs bills above $5,000.
Check with Neris Tal when a property's agreement is missing.
Use the agreement effective when the bill arrived.
Decision requests are due within two business days.
The people named in the request decide in the AP application.
A changed invoice needs a new decision.

A certificate is valid through its expiry day.
Check coverage on the receipt date.
A missing or expired certificate blocks payment but allows entry and approval.
Orla obtains a replacement within two business days.
Retain the earlier check and lift the hold only after checking the supplied certificate.
An invoice signature does not remove an insurance hold.

## 3. Posting and payment

Use the invoice month while it remains open at receipt.
Otherwise use the earliest open month.
Finance supplies the closing day, which is included in the earlier period.
Vendor terms are thirty calendar days from the invoice date unless a separate agreement applies.
Keep the due date while a bill is held.

Payments run each Friday.
Schedule approved bills for the next Friday.

## 4. Finish the work

In the invoice screen, choose **Schedule payment** after all signatures and holds are resolved.
Retain the amount, date, approval references, and confirmation.
Check **Payments** before retrying a missing confirmation.
Finish after a permitted schedule, or record a duplicate, cancellation, or rejection.
