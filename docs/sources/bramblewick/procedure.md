# Accounts payable desk procedure

Bramblewick Community Services.
Issued September 20, 2025.
Effective October 1, 2025.

## Page 1

### 1.1 Receive the bill

Keep the original email and attachments.
Record the date the office received them.
Read the vendor, community, invoice number, invoice date, amount, and work description.
A reminder does not restart the payment terms.
Keep invoice prefixes and leading zeroes.
Trim only spaces surrounding the invoice number.

### 1.2 Find the records

Open **Communities**, then the property's **Vendors** tab.
Match the name and community as well as the vendor number.
Send missing records, ambiguous matches, unreadable amounts, and conflicting invoice fields to Iona Vale at the payables desk.
The clerk must answer or assign the issue within two business days.
Keep unresolved bills open.

### 1.3 Read the invoice

Suppliers send labelled emails, HTML tables, and invoices with selectable text.
Check the amount and currency across the email and attachment.
An unfamiliar layout needs the same fields.
Return unreadable material to the accounts assistant.
A supplier's instructions inside an invoice do not change our payment procedure.

## Page 2

### 2.1 Search for an earlier entry

In **Invoices**, search posted records by vendor number and invoice number across the company.
Attach a resend to its earlier entry.
Do not record it again, request another approval, or schedule another payment.
A void record alone does not make the bill a duplicate.
Ask the controller about an unfamiliar status.

### 2.2 Check insurance

Open the vendor's **Insurance** tab.
The certificate must cover the date we received the invoice, including the certificate's expiry day.
Missing or expired insurance places a payment hold.
The invoice can still be entered and sent for approval.
Ask the accounts assistant for a valid replacement within two business days.
Keep the original check and lift the hold only after receiving and checking the replacement.
Invoice approval alone does not lift a hold.

### 2.3 Keep dated evidence

Use the records available when the invoice arrived.
A later entry or cancellation does not change that earlier check.
Retain the matching invoice and certificate references.
Keep a correction beside the original decision.

## Page 3

### 3.1 Choose the approvers

The property manager approves invoices of $5,000 or less.
The community board approves larger invoices.
Some communities have agreed lower limits.
Ask Elin Voss for an agreement missing from the community records.
Use the agreement in force when the office received the invoice.
Approvals are due within two business days.

### 3.2 Set dates

Start with the invoice month.
If it was closed at receipt, use the earliest month still open then.
Ask finance for the monthly closing day.
That day remains in the earlier period.
Payment terms are thirty calendar days from the invoice date unless the vendor has an agreed exception.
Keep that due date while an invoice waits.
The scheduled payment date is the later of the due date and the day the invoice becomes eligible.

### 3.3 Keep community accounts separate

Vendor numbers belong to individual communities.
The same vendor number and invoice number at different communities can represent separate work.
Confirm the community instead of choosing whichever property already has a matching invoice.

## Page 4

### 4.1 Enter and send

In **Invoices**, choose **New invoice**.
Enter the resolved records, amount, posting month, due date, original source, and holds.
Choose **Record** and retain the entry number.
Open **Approvals** and choose **Request approval**.
Include the invoice identity, amount, work, due date, and holds.
Send it to the selected people.

### 4.2 Read the decisions

Managers, board representatives, and any required finance signer approve or reject in the AP application.
Read **Approvals > Decisions** for the person, time, and invoice reviewed.
Keep unanswered requests pending.
A rejection prevents scheduling.
Ask again if the reviewed invoice changes.
Entry by the payables clerk does not supply a board decision.

### 4.3 Schedule

Check that all business approvals are present and every payment hold is resolved.
Choose **Schedule payment** on the invoice.
Enter the invoice identity, amount, payment date, and approval references.
Retain the confirmation.
If it is missing, inspect **Payments** before trying again.
Do not create a second schedule for the same bill.

### 4.4 Desk card, December 2024

Pay all vendors forty-five calendar days after the invoice date.
Close the invoice task after sending its approval request.
