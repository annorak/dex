# Confirmation of completed service

Bramblewick Community Services.
Issued by Elin Voss on January 15, 2027.

## 1. Receipts from February 1

For invoices above $2,500 received on or after February 1, 2027, obtain the community manager's confirmation that the service was completed.
Request confirmation in the AP application.
The manager responds there within two business days.
Keep payment pending until the confirmation is recorded.
This confirmation is additional to the invoice's required approvals.
Earlier receipts keep their existing approval requirements.
