# Harthmere Court board limit

From Elin Voss, Controller, Bramblewick Community Services.
Issued December 15, 2026.

## 1. Receipts from January 1

For invoices received on or after January 1, 2027, Harthmere Court's board approves amounts greater than $1,800.
The property manager approves amounts of $1,800 or less.
Earlier receipts retain the $1,500 board limit, including invoices still awaiting a decision.
